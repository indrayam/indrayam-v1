A sentiment that I can truly relate to..:-)

Posted : June 3, 2005 at 4:19 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

….I’m the type who likes to stay informed about everything and, until recently, have been holding my own…But that was before blogs, or individual Web logs, picked up popularity….Now everyone is a publisher. There are more than 9 million blogs, and another 40,000 or 50,000 are being created every day….”There are enough blogs to fill up every single second of the day and not be done”…So they win and I lose. It’s more than I can eat….I was considering surrender but realized that would be irresponsible….

— Linda Stern, “Financial blogs multiply”

Amen, Sister! And with that said, I am off again in my daily pursuit of that one nugget, that one podcast or video clip, that one news item which would quench my “info” thirst and help me sleep like a baby tonight, only to start this pursuit all over again come mañana

- Anand
