Ladies and Gentleman, we’ve I-M-P-A-C-T!!

Posted : July 3, 2005 at 11:26 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Wow! For a background on what this is, read the previous entry. So, did the whole thing go well? You decide. So, what did the gurus at Nasa’s Jet Propulsion Lab at Pasadena, CA, think? As one genlteman said, it was “a smashing success…excuse the pun”. Another one remarked, and I paraphrase: “From tomorrow morning, every employee @ JPL (Jet Propulsion Laboratory) will see our solar system differently”

It was nice to share this historic moment with my parents as we all stayed glued to my IBM Thinkpad’s (T42) 14″ LCD screen connected over Wi-Fi to the live feed of NASA TV. Now, wouldn’t it be cool if millions of miles on the other side of this spectacular collision, an “alien” community suddenly witnessed the same event. And as I write this, they’ve probably convened a team of experts already trying to figure out: “What in the world was all that explosion crap?” Or not. Maybe we’re just all alone in this whole goddamn universe. To which I say, “What a waste of space?”

- Anand
