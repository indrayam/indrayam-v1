If India were a stock, would you buy or sell.

Posted : July 5, 2004 at 7:57 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

[via Equitymaster.com]

While the article has many interesting stats, this graph in particular is really interesting and scary at the same time. So, if I read this right, approx. 33% (or 1/3rd) of India’s billion-plus population is under the age of 15!. Forget the economic or political fallout, that’s quite an astounding amount of adrenaline and other hormonal rush for any nation to deal with

On a more serious note, here’s what I think:

Channel this abundant source of youth, their energy and passion the “right” way and India will have nothing but good things to look forward to.

As for the original question - If India were a stock, would I buy or sell - I probably would choose “buy”. But then again, my fiscal astuteness should not be taken seriously as would be obvious to anyone having access to the balance info of my personal Savings Account
