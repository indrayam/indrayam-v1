Difference between you and your boss..:-)

Posted : January 3, 2005 at 5:23 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

[via The Economic Times (forwarded in an email by my dad)]

When you take long time, you’re slow. When your boss takes long, he’s thorough.

When you don’t do it, you’re lazy. When your boss doesn’t do it, he’s too busy.

When you err, you’re a bum. When your boss errs, he’s only human.

When you take a stand, you’re being bull-headed. When your boss does it, he’s being firm.

When you overlook the obvious, you’re being rude. When your boss skips rules, he’s being original.

When you’re out of the office, you’re having fun. When your boss is out of office, he’s on business.
When you step out of bounds, you overstep authority. When your boss does the same, it’s initiative.

- Anand
