Ugh…

Posted : August 25, 2005 at 9:10 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Got an email from my ISP this evening saying that things were not ok with my little “slice” (read, server) on the internet. To make a long story short, this site (and some other sites that I manage) were all dead as a dodo for the better part of the day. My apologies.

Since I am pretty good about keeping this site up, here’s a little tip for you guys in case something like this happens in the future. If you find this site down for more than an hour at a stretch, you can safely conclude that either me or my server is having a really really bad day. Fortunately (or not), this time it was not me, but my poor server. “She” had to put up with a blind and a rather bad date (read, hacker, or should I say “script kiddie”).
