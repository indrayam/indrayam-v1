Ok, so here’s one more thought:

1/(x-8) where x -> 8 would be minus infinity if you thought that x was tending to 8 from the lower side (7.9999999 to 8.0). But, it could just as easily be from the other side (8.0000000… to 8.0). In which case, 1/(x-8) would tend to positive infinity.
