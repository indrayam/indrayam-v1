indrayam.homeip.net!

Posted : January 5, 2005 at 11:40 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Thanks to port forwarding capabilities of my Linksys BEFW11S4 and a little dynamic DNS magic of DynDNS.org, I now have a site (indrayam.homeip.net) serving pages directly from my home workstation!

Oh, and in case the link does not work for you, don’t blame me. Blame SBC. They changed the darn IP on me while I was not looking

Update (01/06/2005 18:58 PST):

Installed 3DWatch client as a service on my HP box @ home. So indrayam.homeip.net should have better uptime now. Ofcourse, it would be nice to host my Apache instance and DynDNS client software on a seperate Linux box, thereby freeing up my HP workstation. Maybe someday soon, but it sure would not be tonight
