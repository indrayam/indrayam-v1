Understanding Google’s guts…

Posted : May 3, 2004 at 8:00 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


[via emergic.org]
Intersting article on Google’s distributed computing platform. Here are some interesting excerpts:

…

  1. 150 million queries/day…
  2. 1,000 queries/sec (peak)
  3. 10,000+ servers
  4. More than 4 tera-ops/sec at daily peak
  5. Index: 3 billion Web pages
  6. 4 billion total docs
  7. 4+ petabytes disk storage

…
Last Thanksgiving, the New York Times reported that Google had crossed the 100,000-server mark. If true, that means Google is operating perhaps the largest grid of computers on the planet. The simple fact that they can build and operate data centers of that size is astounding, says Peter Christy, co-founder of the NetsEdge Research Group, a market research and strategy firm in Silicon Valley.
…
Working at Google, an engineer told me recently, is the nearest you can get to having an unlimited amount of computing power at your disposal.

In some sense, I do envy the guys who work for companies like Google or eBay. Keep up the good work guys. I guess I should have mentioned ‘Google’ web site as a tool in my post as well. What would I be without them?
