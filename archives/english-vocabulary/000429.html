Meaning of the words listed below were taken either from Merriam-Webster’s online site or from dictionary.com, unless otherwise stated.

malefactor (noun):

1: one who commits an offense against the law; especially : FELON
2: one who does ill toward another

Iraq was a military dictatorship with a horrible record of
human rights abuse and a well-earned reputation as an international
malefactor that had attacked its neighbors.

inkhorn (adjective):

ostentatiously learned

If I start using inkhorn words like eructation (a violent belching out or emitting) in my blog entries, chances are I will start losing faithful readers to other bloggers who use simpler words to express themselves.
