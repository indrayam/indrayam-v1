Day 3: In Littleton, CO

Posted : December 23, 2005 at 9:44 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Colorado is beautiful. Oh, and cold. But, I have to say it’s everything that I had thought it would be and much more.

We started from Pueblo, CO today around noon on the final stretch of our journey from Santa Clara, CA to Littleton, CO. And it was a fairly short one. But it was really quite beautiful. Picture a really beautiful and bright sunny day with the sun shining down upon you as fiercely as it can on a winter morning in a piece of land at an elevation of around 5000 ft. Now, imagine the weather outside to be in the 30s. If that feels nice, how about trying to picture snow-capped mountains - Rocky Mountains - in all it’s majestic glory lining the western side of the sky as you are driving up the freeway with patches of snow here and there. Yes, it was quite a beautiful site.
