I’m back…

Posted : August 2, 2004 at 12:14 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

It sure was a wonderful vacation. There were things I had planned on doing during this vacation. Needless to say, I did little of those and instead did the following:

  • Sleep: Lots and lots of it actually
  • Apartment cleanup: Ended up giving a whole slew of clothes to the Salvation Army, which felt good.
  • Shopping (Movies): Bought a few movies for my personal DVD collection.
  • Shopping (Music): I had not used iTunes for some time. Got things going again by purchasing new songs from one of my favorite artist.
  • Played with SkypeOut: Installed the new Skype 1.0 and tried out its new SkypeOut feature. It works pretty well, although making international calls is not quite stable yet.
  • Driving: Aimlessly drove around the Bay Area, something that I tend to enjoy a lot.
  • Movies at AMC: Watched Fahrenheit 9/11 and Harold and Kumar…. Yeah, I know. It’s a wierd combination of movies to see.
  • Couch Potato: I was not aware that the Democratic National Convention was being held the same week (07/25/2004 to 07/31/2004) that I was on vacation. A fairly liberal guy at heart, I stayed glued to the Unconventional Convention Coverage - Hardball Style at MSNBC. “Extreme makeover” charges from our Republican spin-masters aside, I thought it went very well for the Democrats. Of course, I am biased

This may not seem like a very fruitful way of spending time to many. It probably was not my favorite vacation either. However, I feel rested and I could not have asked for more.

Now, where was I …
