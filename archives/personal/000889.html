Practically speaking..;-)

Posted : January 20, 2005 at 1:49 am [America/Los_Angeles]

[tweaked from a post on my dad’s blog]

A Business Analyst and an IT Engineer go on a camping trip, set up their tent, and fall asleep. Some hours later, the IT guy wakes his friend up and asks “Look up at the sky and tell me what you see.”

The Business Analyst replies, “I see millions of stars.”

The IT Engineer asks “What does that tell you?”

The Business Analyst ponders for a minute, and then says “Astronomically speaking, it tells me that there are millions of galaxies and potentially billions of planets. Astrologically, it tells me that Saturn is in Leo. Time wise, it appears to be approximately a quarter past three. Theologically speaking, it’s evident the Lord is all-powerful and we are small and insignificant. Meteorologically speaking, it seems we will have a beautiful day tomorrow.”

“And what does it tell you?”, he asked the IT Engineer.

The IT fella paused and said: “Practically speaking, someone has stolen our tent”.

- Anand
