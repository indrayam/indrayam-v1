“Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish”: A “mantra” that is well worth emulating

Posted : August 28, 2005 at 6:33 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


When I was young, there was an amazing publication called The Whole Earth Catalog, which was one of the bibles of my generation…This was in the late 1960’s, before personal computers and desktop publishing, so it was all made with typewriters, scissors, and polaroid cameras. It was sort of like Google in paperback form, 35 years before Google came along: it was idealistic, and overflowing with neat tools and great notions…Stewart and his team put out several issues of The Whole Earth Catalog, and then when it had run its course, they put out a final issue. It was the mid-1970s, and I was your age. On the back cover of their final issue was a photograph of an early morning country road, the kind you might find yourself hitchhiking on if you were so adventurous. Beneath it were the words: “Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish.” It was their farewell message as they signed off. Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish. And I have always wished that for myself. And now, as you graduate to begin anew, I wish that for you. Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish.

— Steve Jobs, “Commencement address @ Stanford on June 12th, 2005″

Yes, it’s old news. Don’t ask me why I took so long to finally read the commencement speech that took the blog world by storm back when Jobs gave it in June 2005. If you’ve a peaceful five minute in your busy schedule to spare, I would strongly encourage you to read this. It’s good for your soul. And I am not hyperboling a bit.

- Anand
