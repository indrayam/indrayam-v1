On this day 35 years ago: “Houston, Tranquility Base here. The Eagle has landed.”

Posted : July 20, 2004 at 3:15 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Armstrong:   Engine arm is off. [Pause.]
Houston, Tranquility Base here. The Eagle has landed.

The time was 4:18 EDT on July 20, 1969.

Purdue News: Moon Landing Dialogue

Yup, today is the 35th anniversary of that historic day when mankind first set its foot on the Moon. The day when Neil Armstrong uttered those historic first words:

“That’s one small step for a man, one giant leap for mankind.”

A few years later, an entirely different kind of Eagle landed, albeit on Earth this time. Translation: I was born!
