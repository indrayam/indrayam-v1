A poetic reminder to myself

Posted : December 15, 2005 at 12:05 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Breathe

What adventure am I on?

I know not.

The tide takes me where ever it must,

I’m not in control.



The spray from the water blinds me,

I can not see beyond.

What lies ahead?

I have to trust and wait.



I am a mystery to myself

as life is a mystery to the water.

Will I survive the waves as they crash on the shore?

Or will I master the flow?



Patience is the key



Breathe…Breathe

Someone I know (and will hopefully talk about here some day) wrote this. Enjoy!

- Anand
