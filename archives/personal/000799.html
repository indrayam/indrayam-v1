Posted : November 9, 2004 at 1:32 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

As a result of this, the school’s garden and landscape has become overgrown and out of control. They would like to coordinate a project with our Cisco volunteers to help beautify their school grounds. This will involve some weeding, raking, and general yard clean-up.
