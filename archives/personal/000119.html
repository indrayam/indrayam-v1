Confession..:-)

Posted : May 18, 2004 at 9:43 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Jokes aside, I really do have a confession to make. So, here I go:

This past weekend, I decided to launch two linkblogs - one on Tech stuff and the other on India. Well, as it turns out, I was being a bit naive thinking that I’d be able to run two linkblogs, my own blog, read all the RSS feeds coming my way thanks to you and do justice to my day-time job all without a few good tools in place for me to be more productive, especially vis-a-vis maintaining linkblogs.

Frankly, the Tech linkblog is really for myself. With all the good technology related linkblogs out there, I really don’t think there is anything special that mine would bring to the table. Of course, I would not stop you from tuning in if you like the flavor of links that I select

I am more excited about Induspolis.com. This linkblog will aggregate issues (good or bad) that are being discussed online about India. It could be by Indians, but it really does not have to be that way. If you happen to be writing about your experiences about India or Indians, I’d be interested.

So stay tuned and accept my apologies for the inconvenience these changes may have caused
