Do you know anybody who actually blogs and is in their 60s (if not older)

Posted : May 2, 2004 at 11:46 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


Well, as it turns out, both me and my dad started blogging at the same time. This evening my brother suddenly remarked “Dad might very well be one of the (if not the) first Indian blogger who is 65 years old”. That got both of us thinking and as expected, the next remark that he blurted out was “Is he the first active blogger who is 65 years old?”. Hmm..

So, my question to you is: “Do you know anybody who actively blogs and is in their 60s, 70s, 80s..Ok, you get the idea . Just curious.

- Anand

People in their 60’s, 70’s, and 80’s grew up in a time when you wrote your thoughts in diaries, and those diaries were burnt or buried with you when you died. Today, people fall in love and divorce on TV, reveal their deepest feelings on talk shows, and engage in mom-swaps for the sake of fame (or infamy). Yes, I believe that blogging is for the young.
