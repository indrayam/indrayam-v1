Doing business in India is like…:-)

Posted : July 19, 2005 at 6:20 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

There is a common saying in India that the humble coconut tree is of no use for the first seven years of its life. Thereafter, almost every part of it can be made use of. Doing business in India is very much the same. You need patience to learn the way things are done and to not give up before your efforts bear fruit.

— “And in the East, too, a red carpet”, Indian Express

Btw, while we’re on the subject of doing business in India, the latest Fortune Issue covers the topic of how to invest in India: Six smart ways to invest in India. You may need subscription to read the online article. I would suggest picking up the issue on the stands at your nearest bookstore and perusing through it quickly. It’s not too long an article, but definitely has a few nuggets.

- Anand
