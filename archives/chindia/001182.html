Do we have comedy clubs in India?

Posted : January 23, 2006 at 11:30 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Went to see “Looking for Comedy in the Muslim World” yesterday and thoroughly enjoyed it. However, the movie brought up a point that I was kinda surprised to hear: Are there any comedy clubs in New Delhi? Are there comedy clubs anywhere in India?

As a kid, I remember watching “Hasya Kavi Sammelan” and getting a really good laugh from those. And ofcourse, it’s not like we have a dearth of jokes in our country, thanks to fictional characters like Santa Singh and Banta Singh. However, I still need to know if we have a concept of comedy clubs where standup comedians entertain crowds on a daily or weekly basis. Any inputs would be much appreciated.

- Anand
