Learn Chinese?

Posted : July 18, 2005 at 6:37 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

A couple of ideas I have been chewing on over the weekend…I spent this weekend in Des Moines, Iowa. I was there to give a talk to the National Governors Association annual meeting on importance of high-speed communications networks for economic growth in their states…I told them we have 2 choices, learn to compete for capital or learn Chinese. How to grow your state? Simple: make your state a destination resort for capital and the jobs and paychecks will follow.

— Dr. John Rutledge, “Guest Blogger”
