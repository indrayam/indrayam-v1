xogiving.org: If you’re in US/Canada, you may find this cause worthwhile

Posted : November 12, 2007 at 8:04 pm [America/Los_Angeles]






More details here.

If you’ve not heard of OLPC (One Laptop Per Child), you may want to check it out. Here’s how they define their goal on their web site:

To provide children around the world with new opportunities to explore, experiment and express themselves

Anyways, to make a long story short, I have been waiting to get my hands on one of these for a long time. So, when I found out that I could actually do that and in the process help a child somewhere get their hands on an amazing educational toy, it was a no-brainer. Note: I should really thank my wife for this, as she was equally passionate about this project and that made the purchase somewhat easier..

The sale ends in two weeks.
