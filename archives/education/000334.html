Posted : June 29, 2004 at 10:39 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

I think it’s really interesting how a simple error page could potentially be used in a “Data Structures 101″ class to illustrate a data structure such as a struct containing arrays of struct and how such a data structure is used in this particular case to display real world data.
