Planning to switch over to ServerMatrix Hosting in the next few days

Posted : April 30, 2004 at 12:16 am [America/Los_Angeles]


My current server hosting provider is JohnCompanies. I must say that I am thoroughly impressed with the company, especially “John”. However, thanks to the fact that I have started blogging in all earnestness and so has my dad (not to mention a few more folks waiting in the wings to jump right in), I have somewhat outgrown the machine that I am currently on. Have already lined up my new server over to ServerMatrix. It’s a wee bit costlier, but I think it’s well worth the money. Here are the specs:

Server: Super Server 2.4 GHz
Primary HDD: 80GB Hard Drive
Secondary HDD: None
RAM: 512 MB RAM
Number of ips: 5 IP Addresses
Bandwidth: 1000 GB Bandwidth
Uplink Port Speed: 100 Mbps Uplink - Monthly Fee: $10.00

Should be a lot of work and fun. Plan to complete the move latest by next Tuesday. So, if things look flaky over the weekend vis-a-vis the site, now you know why
