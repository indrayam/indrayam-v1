2008 US Presidential Elections

Posted : January 20, 2007 at 10:11 am [America/Los_Angeles]

It’s going to be interesting to see what the citizens of this country decide in 2008. I was not impressed with their choice in 2000 and 2004, to put it mildly. Will I be disappointed again? I have a feeling I just might be in for a pleasant surprise. I can’t wait for it.

With that said, we still have two more years to go. And for the sake of this country and the entire world, I can only hope and pray that these turn out to be President George W. Bush’s finest years in office.
