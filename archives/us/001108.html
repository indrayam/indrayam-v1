Rush is Right?

Posted : September 12, 2005 at 8:27 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


I got a sneaking suspicion that the left’s reaction to this (Katrina’s aftermath) is something that they had in the works ever since 9/11. I think the left has been waiting for the next terrorist attack. They’ve got their battle plan in motion, and they knew when the terrorist attack came that they were going to jump on Bush’s case as being ill-prepared, unprepared, lousy, having done nothing, make the case for bigger government, roll out all the video of all the disasters and misery,” and so forth and so on. And I think one of the reasons they were able to gin up so fast is they’ve had this in the works. They’ve had it in the works and they’ve tried and they’ve grown impatient. There’s just something about communist thugs that brings a smile to the face of an American lefty and makes their hearts go pitter patter…And then there’s…let’s see. Oh! Did I mention the drop in the President’s poll numbers? Yes, these are heady days for our left wing friends…

— Rush Limbaugh, “Rush is Right: The Left Celebrates Katrina Destruction, Terror Attack They’ve Been Waiting For”

I guess I should really find some time to study the history of the two primary parties of United States. This is N-U-T-S!! What in God’s name would make someone say something like this. I can’t speak for all the “lefties” out there Mr. Limbaugh, but I can definitely speak for myself. And it’s really the same three words that I have often repeated on my blog - “God Bless America”.

- Anand
