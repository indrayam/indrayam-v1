Somewhere along the way you ended up disliking your country…:-(

Posted : April 1, 2007 at 2:00 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Not all of the content referenced here, nearly two decades ago, survived the years.

With all due respect to all concerned, to hell with you Mr. O’Reilly. Personally, I don’t think it serves any purpose for us to sit around and pass judgements about others. I mean, really. Why in God’s name do I care if Iranian leaders acted like scumbags? For the record, I do think that the Iranians acted like scumbags. Their actions have been deplorable, to say the least. That said, as much as I would have hoped for a more mature reaction from Iran and I am disappointed by it all, I am not surprised. Why should I? It’s not like the world looks up to the Iranian leaders for moral leadership.

And that’s what your puny little head failed to understand and will perhaps never do. As someone who loves this nation dearly, I care more about the moral standing of this nation. That’s what really matters to me. That’s what the former colonel was trying to point out. And you had the audacity to pass judgement on her service to this nation. You had the nerve to suggest that somewhere along the way she had started disliking her own country. Thanks Billo. With friends like you right here at home, who needs more enemies.

- Anand
