What in the world..

Posted : September 9, 2004 at 3:53 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Why in the freaking world are we talking about Bush’s National Guard Service 54 days before Presidential Elections ^%@&*$#@@#$

Are the two sides completely incapable of having a civil debate over issues? Come on guys. There are young kids out there in Iraq dying. Two every day! Billions of dollars have already gone down this black hole called Iraq. Al Qaeda continues to behead any American (Democrat or Republican) that it can get its dirty hands on. Economy could do better. Good jobs are few and far between. Poverty has risen. Health Care is anything but health care. Environment is being treated as a step-child.

And look around the so-called liberal blogs and newspapers and all you will see is “AWOL” or “National Guard”? How is this any better than those jerks on radio (or cable news) who think they are pundits in politics talking endlessly about Kerry’s extent of war wounds.

This is nuts!

- Anand
