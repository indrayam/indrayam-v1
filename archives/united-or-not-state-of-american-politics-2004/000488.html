Here’s my mantra for anyone who is serious about wanting to help Senator Kerry’s platform:

1. Be optimistic. This is the key! You cannot fight a good fight when you’re feeling depressed.
2. Clearly define what you can do between now and Nov. 2nd for Senator Kerry.
3. Just do it!

Just as a frame of reference, here’s what I have decided on doing:

1. Blog (using ‘facts’ with Sources identified) why President Bush’s record on National Security, Economy and Environment is NOT a very good one.
2. Read Blogs and Books to stay informed. However, never follow any information and/or opinion blindly. Make up your own mind.
3. Identify 3-5 people in my sphere of influence who seem to be not sure if they want to vote this Fall. Encourage them to register and make sure they vote (hopefully for Senator Kerry)!

Notice that I purposely don’t plan on talking much about Senator Kerry’s platform. I know he has a lot of good ideas. However, I really think that in order for Senator Kerry to win, we must first dispell the myths floating around about President Bush’s records.
