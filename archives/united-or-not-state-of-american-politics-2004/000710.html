Who will win on Nov. 2nd?

Posted : October 25, 2004 at 10:53 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Yesterday, while spending quality time with my family, my elder brother brought up this all important question, “Who will win on Nov. 2nd?”. I was the first to jump in and proclaim that Senator Kerry will win. No ifs and buts. While some agreed with me, I think my elder brother was not so sure. Needless to say, that got me a trifle worried.

Am I guilty of the same thing as folks who watch “Fox News” everyday? Am I living in my own little virtual “cocoon” (blogs) surrounded by opinions from like-minded individuals who are really a minority in this country? I would like to believe otherwise. I would like to believe that I am part of a huge grassroots campaign made popular by people like Joe Trippi while working for Gov. Howard Dean. A campaign that in the end would dethrone a powerful, well-connected and stubborn Administration come Nov. 2nd.

Having said that, I am also a pragmatist. Anyone who has read my blog these past few months knows that I would like Senator Kerry/Senator Edwards team to win. However, I have also stated too many times on this blog that in the end “On Nov. 3rd, Americans will get a President that they deserve”.

If President Bush and Vice President Cheney win, it will mean two things:

  • the lack of a real “fair and balanced” Media continues to fail the people of this country and will be a major problem for the very fabric of this country’s democracy in the years to come
  • the “Democrats” are dead! If they can’t win this time around, it will take a miracle (or a Hillary Clinton) for them to win the Presidency again

I don’t expect the next President of the United States to be perfect. However, I do expect the next President to be someone who is willing to acknowledge and learn from his mistakes. I do expect the next President to truly be a “uniter”, someone who will reach out to “all” Americans - blacks, whites, gays, lesbians, theists or atheists. I do expect the next President to champion the cause of advancement of science and technology and not cite excuses to curtail its advancement. I do expect the next President to understand that while 9/11 did change many things, it has not changed the basic axioms on which this country was founded and has prospered all these years. And lastly, but not the least, I do expect the next President to never ever play politics with the lives of humans - Americans or non-Americans, Civilians or Soldiers. It is imperative for the next President of the United States to understand that “a preemptive act of aggression alone cannot be a solution to any problem, let alone the current problem of terrorism”. Violence begets violence. It’s that simple.

So, who will win on Nov. 2nd? Actually, I really don’t know. And that’s what makes the next 7 days so damn hard

- Anand
