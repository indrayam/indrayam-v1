This election season DOES NOT start until Sep. 3rd, 2004!

Posted : August 31, 2004 at 8:30 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

A couple of minutes on any liberal bloggers site and you can’t miss comments from conscientious democrats that goes something like this - “Oh jeez. We’re in trouble” or “JK needs to come out and fight like a MAN!” or something equally or more asinine. Get a grip guys. What did you expect? President Bush and his team would come to NY and hand Senator Kerry the keys to the White House!. Or, that the Republican (or any) Party would suddenly decide to play it fair during their Convention?

Here’s my advice:

If you believe in what you’re doing, if you really really believe that President Bush is NOT the answer for United States of America come Nov. 2nd 2004, then put your heads down, work hard and work smart. Oh, and please do not forget a BIG slice of humor while you go about fighting this good fight.

I may be wrong, but I still think that mega shows such as big huge conventions don’t win elections. You had your chance and you did well! Now, it’s their chance. Let’em do what they think is best for their party and their platform.

Let the spinners spin, the media manipulate, the pundits puke. This election season DOES NOT start until Sep. 3rd, 2004.

- Anand
