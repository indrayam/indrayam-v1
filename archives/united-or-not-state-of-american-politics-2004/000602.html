Senator Kerry, whatever you do tomorrow, DO NOT..

Posted : September 29, 2004 at 1:04 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

…play nice for the sake of playing nice. If you’re mad, wait for your turn and then channel your rage into words as best as you can. If you’re attacked, attack back. Speak from your heart. Don’t think too much about what others might think if you did this or that. If you feel like saying something, say it. You’ve nothing to lose and everything to win.

There are Americans dying out there in a different country and the country could care less about their would-be Commander-in-Chief being nice. The country needs a Leader. I know you have it in you to be a great Leader.

So, relax, wait for the right moment and go for the kill. Lord knows that a ’silent and growing majority’ of this great country will be rooting for you tomorrow.

- Anand
