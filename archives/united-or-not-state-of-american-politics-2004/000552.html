I have tried my best to not get sucked into it, but…

Posted : September 21, 2004 at 8:48 am [America/Los_Angeles]

..it seems to me like there are so many pathetic and useless distractions in this all-important Election.

  • Swift Boat Veterans for Truth and their concoctions about how Senator Kerry’s Medals were not well deserved
  • Personal attacks on Senator Kerry. Calling him a flip-flopper, wishy-washy
  • President Bush’s National Guard Service
  • RatherGate
  • CBS, Bill Burkett and Joe Lockhart planned and unleashed the “Killian” memos

    and now this..

  • Kerry Fund-Raisers Met With S. Korean

I think it’s time to remind ourselves what an-ex President recently said about winning Presidential Elections. Frankly, this Presidential Election has been quite an eye-opener for me and gave me a better understanding of how US politics works. Frankly, I am not terribly impressed..

Spinsanity, indeed!
