Mr. President, I have had it with you!

Posted : August 26, 2004 at 11:13 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

It was a mentally exhausting day for me, and upon returning home, I came across two different sets of news items which just left me speechless. So, if I sound unduly harsh, accept my apologies. But for once, I could care less. I have had it with this country’s politics, the majority of its spineless political leaders (who supposedly “serve” the public) and lastly but not the least, the pathetic partisan media.

2004 Iraq deaths now exceed 2003 deaths

It happened this week almost without notice: The number of Americans killed in Iraq during 2004 now exceeds the number killed in 2003.

More remarkably, the 488 killed thus far this year died in just 239 days (2.04 daily average), while the 482 killed last year died during fully 287 days (1.68 daily average), which means that not only has 2004 been bloodier than 2003 in absolute terms, but in relative terms as well.

…
Now, here’s the question I really want to ask: How long will it take the media to report this indisputable fact? I mean, can they take even five minutes time from the latest, breathless Swift Boat twist to report the fact that more Americans have died in Iraq this year than last?

Daily Kos: 2004 Iraq deaths now exceed 2003 deaths

It’s a funny thing, this human nature. You take away 1 or 2 human life per day and people will gradually get so immune to it that they will not even notice it, even if it continued for almost two years. Makes me wonder if the reaction of this nation and its feelings towards their President would be the same if these 970 American lives were snatched away from us by terrorists on a single day! Makes you wonder if we still would hold no one accountable (besides the perpetrators) for such a failure (in a post 9/11 world ) and would continue to let wimps on television who never volunteered to serve in any war in any capacity talk endlessly about Swift Boat Veterans and their (g)utter concoctions about John Kerry on their allegedly “fair and balanced” brain-dead shows.

Somehow, this tragedy and loss of life is not “registering” with the folks who live here. Maybe it’s because these soldiers are getting killed and maimed far far away from our peaceful life here in United States. Or, maybe, it’s because these 970 caskets were never shown on “our” television sets while we were having dinner with our familes. I don’t know and I can’t seem to figure this one out. It just does not “register” with these people that there are 970 fathers and mothers who’ll never get their sons or daughters back. Period. It does not “register” with these people that there are 7000 families right now somewhere in United States where a luved one went from being the caretaker, the bread earner to being the one who is taken care of 24×7 just like that. It does not “register” with these people that these kids did not die fighting an imminent threat for this country. They fought a war where the “goal” kept changing colors like a chameleon and based on the direction of the political winds blowing through the country.

Why aren’t people realizing that we went to fight a pre-emptive war in Iraq because of the threat of WMD, because this administration felt Saddam was an imminent threat to the borders of this nation. It was NEVER, NOT ONCE because President Bush’s administration, after reading umpteen CNN news articles about the torture of innocent Iraqis under Saddam’s regime, said - “Gosh! This is terrible. We should help the Iraqis.” That is just not true. The name “pre-emptive war” tells the story. If this war would have been about liberating Iraqis, it would have been called “Iraqi Liberation War” or something like that. We went in because our intelligence failed us. We went in because someone chose to trust a scumbag like Ahmad Chalabi rather than seek the truth the old-fashioned CIA way. We went in because, instead of heeding to the advice of the millions both here and around the world, our President chose to stubbornly “stay the course” and lead the nation to a war in the name of “War on Terrorism”, “WMD’s imminent threat”, “Saddam ignored UN inspections, so we need to fix it”, “add your favorite reason here”, “add more of your favorite reasons here”.

Citizens of this nation, indeed the whole world, rallied and marched among other things to make their voices heard. Elderly statesman from around the world warned. Heck, even the Pope said it was not the right thing to do. Yet, this intellectually challenged President stubbornly said “I respectfully disagree, but I think I know better”. And today, when all of us have been proven right, no one in this current administration have been held accountable. Not the Commander-in-Chief. Not the Secretary of State. Not the Defense Secretary. Not the Vice President. Just one “CIA” director, and that too after resisting it for months. Well, Mr. President, allow me to “respectfully” respond to your “respectful disagreement” with us:

The world, the Pope, the elders of this country and the whole world were right. You were wrong! And you must be held accountable, come Nov. 2nd, 2004

And today, 69 days before the elections, I also read this in the news:

Bush Admits Iraq “miscalculations” - NY Times


President Bush (news - web sites) acknowledged for the first time on Thursday that he had miscalculated post-war conditions in Iraq (news - web sites), the New York Times reported.

…

The president also discussed the issue of North Korea (news - web sites) and Iran’s nuclear ambitions, saying that he would not be rushed to
set deadlines.

Yahoo: Bush Admits Iraq ‘Miscalculations’ - NY Times

Why could you not have shown the same patience with Iraq. Is North Korea or Iran any less evil in your book or any less threatening than Iraq was in 2002? I bet it is not. So why are you so patient now? I think I know the answer. I know you’re a good man Mr. President and I also know you’re a man of faith. I am willing to wager a bet that deep down in your heart today, you know you rushed into Iraq and led us and the world into a war which could have been done differently. You know there was more that you could have done. After all, it was you (not the Congress, not Dick Cheney, not Donald Rumsfeld, and certainly not John Kerry) who gave the marching orders to US military to invade Iraq on that fateful day in 2002. Deep down in your heart Mr. President, you know you were wrong. And that’s why you need to hold yourself accountable.

Jeez..what a difference 537 votes can make in the future of the world!

- Anand
