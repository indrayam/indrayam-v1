I challenge every PATRIOTIC American to watch this. NOW!

Posted : September 1, 2004 at 8:36 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

To my knowledge, no other documentary (sorry Mr. Michael Moore) even comes close to capturing the nonsensical insanity that was let lose on the citizens of this nation via every possible medium (airwaves, op-eds, books) which ultimately culminated in US invading Iraq.

I don’t think I would need to coerce my liberal friends into watching this unless they’ve already seen it. And to “all” my conservative brethren who “truly” believe that the Swift Boat Veterans deserved to be heard, well, the men and women in and behind the making of this documentary are no less patriotic towards their country.

Infact, I challenge every American (including the Hannitys, the Limbaughs, the Savages, the Coulters, the O’Reillys, the Zahns and you-know-who-you-are) to watch this. And if after watching all the 87 or so minutes of it, you still come out thinking that all of us who still question President Bush’s means and motives of going into the Iraq war are nothing but crazed liberals out to “bring down” a great President, I have nothing more to say.

- Anand
