A Remarkable Promise

Posted : October 29, 2004 at 1:09 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


Click to play the movie

This biographical movie shown at the Democratic National Convention shows family and friends talking about the courage John Kerry has shown as a soldier and a veteran, the fights he has waged for middle-class values and the faith in family he has exhibited as a father and husband.

If you don’t have the time to see Going Upriver and still would like to know a little about John Kerry, I think this documentary does a pretty good job.
