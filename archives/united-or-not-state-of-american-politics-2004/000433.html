With 75 days to go, what are the two campaigns talking about?

Posted : August 18, 2004 at 10:38 am [America/Los_Angeles]


Splash page on John Kerry’s site (as on 08/18/2004)


Splash page on George Bush’s site (as on 08/18/2004)

Now, carefully listen to all of this. See a pattern here.

The President of the United States, the war president, the peace president, the “compassionate conservative” of 2000, the charming fella from Texas, the “I hear you and the whole world hears you” conscientious and caring President, the straight shooter, one who leads with a steady hand and moral clarity - this is the best plan that you and your party have to offer Americans as a reason to vote for you come Nov. 2nd:

Smear Kerry. Go after his Vietnam record. His 20 years of Senate record. Never mind my record as a Governor of the great state of Texas for 8 years and my record as the President of the United States for 4 years. Just focus on smearing Kerry. Keep throwing more adjectives at him than one can possibly throw with the bazillions in our re-election war chest. Bottomline, discredit John Kerry’s character to the point where nothing that he says would matter.

That’s it. That’s the grandiose plan for our Commander-in-Chief, George W. Bush, to (barely) win another election.

What a pity

- Anand
