Image of the night (before I crash)..:-)

Posted : August 25, 2004 at 12:55 am [America/Los_Angeles]

John Kerry appeared on “The Daily Show with Jon Stewart” tonight (08/24/2004). Here are my observations:

  • Let’s not kid ourselves: He ain’t no Bill Clinton! Not that there is anything wrong with it.
  • He appeared exhausted
  • However, his responses were sharp and smart as always and
  • Kerry looked pretty relaxed and comfortable in his own skin during the show

Seems like a conscientious, learned, Vietnam war veteran (”war-hero” to many), “frost-bite” liberal (as his Republican opponents would brand him as) old fella from Masachusettes to me. Frankly, that sounds like a fine cocktail of virtues for the next United States President and Leader of the Free World

GO KERRY!

- Anand
