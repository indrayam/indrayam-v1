MIT and Caltech voting experts share..

Posted : September 22, 2004 at 5:12 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

[via MIT and Caltech voting experts announce seven steps to ensure your vote is counted]

…seven recommendations to ensure your vote gets counted:

  1. Make sure you are registered.
  2. Get a sample ballot from your local elections office.
  3. Bring your sample ballot to help you in the voting booth.
  4. Try to vote between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m.
  5. Know your rights and ask for help if you need it.
  6. You have a right to vote if you are registered
  7. Stop and double-check that your ballot reflects how you want to vote
