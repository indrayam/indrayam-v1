“Sphere of Influence” - A tangible way to help Kerry/Edwards ticket..:-)

Posted : September 5, 2004 at 12:55 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Since I don’t get to vote in the upcoming Presidential Elections, I decided to look at what I fondly call my “sphere of influence” and see if I can add “new” voters in Kerry/Edwards column. What do I mean by “new” voters? Well, these are voters who have never voted before or did not vote for Al Gore in 2000.

I am trying to talk the daughters of one of my closest friends into registering and voting during this upcoming elections

If I execute well, here’s what it would mean for Senator Kerry:

At least +2 “new” voters for him in Arizona

At least +1 “new” voter for him in California

I know it’s not much. However, it’s a start. Come to think of it, it may not be a bad idea to use your “sphere of influence” to do the same.

These +1s do add up in the end!
