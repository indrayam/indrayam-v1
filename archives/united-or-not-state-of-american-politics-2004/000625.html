Vice President Cheney’s blatant lies and misstatements from last night

Posted : October 6, 2004 at 11:48 am [America/Los_Angeles]

  1. “Your hometown newspaper has taken to calling you Senator Gone.”
  2. “Now, in my capacity as vice president, I am the president of Senate, the presiding officer. I’m up in the Senate most Tuesdays when they’re in session.”
  3. I am seeing you (Senator Edwards) for the first time tonight
  4. “The senator has got his facts wrong. I have not suggested there’s a connection between Iraq and 9/11.”
  5. Saddam Hussein having established link with Al Qaeda and George Tenet talking about a 10-year relationship
  6. “The allies have stepped forward and agreed to reduce and forgive Iraqi debt to the tune of nearly $80 billion, by one estimate.”
  7. Kerry’s tax-cut rollback would hit 900,000 small businesses.
  8. Cheney continued to charge that Kerry voted 98 times to raise taxes.
  9. Cheney charged that Kerry and Edwards oppose the No Child Left Behind education law

I think Don Imus said it best this morning when he said, “The Vice President spent 90 minutes lying.” Tell me about it.

I think what’s a real shocker to me is that he lied more to be mean towards Senator Kerry and Senator Edwards. I would understand if he would have lied or exaggerated to pump up his and President Bush’s record. Oh well, I guess their record is just beyond repair!
