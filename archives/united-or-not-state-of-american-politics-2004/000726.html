Tony Fabrizio of Fabrizio/McLaughlin & Associates, a Republican pollster, says it all

Posted : October 27, 2004 at 2:39 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Here’s his latest analysis of the Battleground States and the latest analysis of the Newly Registered Voters.

In a nutshell, it says:

If Minorities and first time registered voters turn out, Kerry will win a landslide.

I think Tony hit the nail on the head when he concluded:

Given the age, ideology, partisan identification, and vote proclivity of these new voters, it is clear that John Kerry owes George Soros, MTV and ACT big time for keeping him competitive in this race. This data suggests that the GOP’s hope of being competitive with the Democrats in terms of new registrants was not much more than a dashed hope.

I think you saw a sample of this in my previous post.
