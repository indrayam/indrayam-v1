Gertie Walker, 105 year old, votes!

Posted : October 24, 2004 at 1:28 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


Gertie Walker, 105 years old, at her home
in Centerville. She’s cast her ballot since women
first won the right to vote.

CENTERVILLE — Gertie Walker celebrated her birthday Oct. 12 in a traditional way: She had lunch with friends at a restaurant and spent the afternoon reading congratulatory cards and receiving other friends who visited her West Hackberry Street home.

The next day, which was the first day of early voting in the presidential election, Miss Gertie, as her friends know her, kept another tradition: She cast her ballot.

When you are 105, you don’t put off these things.

…

Voting precious to Miss Gertie, and has been since 1920

Gertie, you rock!!
