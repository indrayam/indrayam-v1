A Uniter as a President is always good…

Posted : September 8, 2004 at 4:24 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Gallup asked:


Thinking about the following characteristics and qualities, please say whether you think each one applies more to John Kerry or more to George W. Bush:

Will unite the country and not divide it.

Gallup’s analysis:

After the Democratic convention, John Kerry had a significant lead over George W. Bush on the question of which candidate will unite the country and not divide it. Following the Republican convention, the two candidates are essentially tied.

My analysis:

Considering that after all the “goodies” bestowed upon him during the RNC Senator Kerry still led President Bush in this category (never mind the margin) means one thing:

Senator Kerry is better at uniting the American people than President Bush

Lord knows this country needs a Uniter!

- Anand
