The genius of American conservative radio talk shows

Posted : August 31, 2004 at 3:35 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

RUSH LIMBAUGH:

Could it possibly be that the root of all of this anger at the U.S. can be traced to the Vietnam War and perhaps what John Kerry said himself? With all these stories of decapitation and dismemberment and burning villages and shooting people in the back, could it be that John Kerry so deceives himself with his anti-war activism for some of the dislike or hatred that exists for America today among certain people around the world? I think it’s distinctly possible. I think this man is largely responsible for a lot of things that he claims to be, uh, better suited to fix than anyone else is. I just think this is all coming home to roost.

…

“They were panicked on MSNBC after Giuliani spoke, because Giuliani just literally dissected John Kerry as though he was a frog in the junior high chemistry class.”

…

“If you look at Kerry’s foreign policy it’s all about pleasing somebody else, not us. It’s always about pleasing the French, making the Germans happy, the European elites or whoever. It’s not possible. It’s a failed policy, it’s a failed behavioral trait, and it doesn’t accomplish anything. You have to be a phony in order to pull something like that off.”

…

“Kids in the Middle East are born, and they’re taught hate from the moment they’re put into schools, and what amazes me is the cynicism that Bush’s vision of liberty and freedom is greeted with by his opponents.”

…

“I know you’re not supposed to judge people by the way they look, walk, or talk. I’m not judging Edwards, but I feel like I’m listening to Gomer Pyle in street clothes. I’m sorry. I know it probably shouldn’t be said.”

…

Rush Limbaugh…

And to think that this guy is faithfully heard by millions of Americans on a daily basis certainly speaks volumes about the “quality” of the dialogue (or monologue I guess) that pervades the airwaves of this country every day, not to say anything about the “quality” of the participants of such a show.

To use Captain Haddock’s many pet outbursts, “Billions of bilious blue blistering barnacles”.

- Anand
