It almost feels like the quiet before the Storm..

Posted : October 21, 2004 at 1:14 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Wonder what Karl Rove is cooking these days in that puny little head of his.

As much as he luvs to see that the Mary Cheney and the Teresa Heinz Kerry episodes are being religiously played up by his Media brethren, he knows he needs something more explosive.

Something like:

  • Zarqawi caught… Or,
  • Mullah Mohammad Omar (the Taliban guy) caught… Or, the mother of all,
  • Osama Bin Laden caught…

Ofcourse, knowing his track record, he might be just as content with things like

  • Senator Kerry and Jane Fonda had a luv child… Or,
  • Senator Edwards and Paris Hilton caught kissing…

Then again, he might be putting finishing touches on something even more diabolical: “How-to Steal the Elections in 11 days!”

Frankly, the way I see it, he could care less. Anything, just about anything that he can get his hands on to bring down the two Johns! And don’t you dare make the mistake of counting him out yet. He still has 11 days. That’s a lot of time for him to annhilate his opponent!

As Andrew Sullivan would say, the guys is “poisonous, but effective”.

- Anand
