Election Day has arrived!

Posted : September 19, 2004 at 9:55 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

[via MyDD: The Election Day has Arrived]

In many states, including Iowa and Oregon, voting begins this week. By October 18th, four weeks from tomorrow, voting will have begun in a majority of swing states

…

In 2000, over 15% of the electorate voted before Election Day. This year, that percentage will increase significantly

…

Kerry needs to improve on Gore’s performance in early voting. According to the National Annenberg Election Survey, Bush took a sizable lead into Election Day in 2000. In fact, the lead was so sizable that Gore defeated Bush on Election Day by at least 2,500,000 votes, and overcame deficits in Oregon, Iowa, Wisconsin, New Mexico and Florida.

Ok, let’s roll folks.
