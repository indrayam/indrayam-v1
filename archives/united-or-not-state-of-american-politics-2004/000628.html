US was “almost all wrong”on weapons

Posted : October 7, 2004 at 9:20 am [America/Los_Angeles]

[via Washington Post: U.S. ‘Almost All Wrong’ on Weapons]

How could this happen? The more I think about it, the more clearer it gets. This was not a colossal “Intelligence” failure as some in this current Administration would have us believe. Indeed, this was a “colossal error in judgement” which has made this world a more dangerous place than it ever was. To all those alleged pundits on 24×7 Cable NewsChannel and Radio Airwaves, here’s what I have to say, “You all have blood on your hands. Blood of US soldiers, Coalition soldiers, Iraqi soldiers, Iraqi civilians and civilians in other countries who died in terrorist attacks since the start of Iraq War.”.

I don’t care if it sounds too harsh. You deserve it. You caved in under pressure from this Administration and played along with their neocon agenda. You failed us. If you think US Elections of 2000 was your biggest failure, think again.

- Anand
