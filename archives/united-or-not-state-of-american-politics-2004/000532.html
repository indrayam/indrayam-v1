Undecided about upcoming Presidential Elections

Posted : September 18, 2004 at 11:34 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Well, here’s how I see it:

Either you and “your” America ARE better off today than four years ago or NOT.

If you think things are better, please vote for President George Bush & Vice President Dick Cheney come Nov. 2nd, 2004.

If you think things are worse, then..

  • Please don’t let some pundit tell you that everything (the Economy, the Tax cuts, the Deficits, the Environmental policies, the Education policies) that is wrong today is owing to 9-11
  • Please don’t let somone else’s asinine personal attacks (dumb, liar, flip-flopper, namby pamby) drive your opinion about a candidate
  • Please don’t let folks lead you into believing that things will be better off in the NEXT four years.

Start planning on holding folks accountable for their actions this past four years come Nov. 2nd. Please vote for Senator John Kerry & Senator John Edwards

A word of caution:

Try not to let the personal bias of Online sites (such as this one), Radio talkshow hosts, Polls, Pollsters, Poll results, Cable and Television News anchors, even your over-zealous neighbor get in your way of “clear” thinking. It is difficult, sometimes even impossible.

One way to tackle this is to try to get information from as many sources as possible before you let yourself form an opinion. Once you form your opinion, try to stay away from the partisan shows and web sites as much as you possible can. Trust me, they don’t help.

No matter what you do, please DO NOT do the following:

  • Not vote at all
  • Vote for Ralph Nader or others (besides President Bush and Senator Kerry). In my humble opinion, it’s as good as NOT voting.

All the best

- Anand
