Mr. Vice President: If Kerry’s use of the words “sensitive war” is difficult for you to digest, digest this…

Posted : August 12, 2004 at 7:17 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

[via Cheney criticizes call for ‘more sensitive’ war]


Click on the image to see the movie

“Our enemies are innovative and resourceful, and so are we. They never stop thinking about new ways to harm our country and our people, and neither do we.” - George W. Bush, Washington, D.C., Aug. 5, 2004

Now, I think you’re a smart man, Mr. Vice President, to realize that you should not waste your and our time with such word games. Lord only knows how your friend (our President) would fare if we were to start digging up archives of improper (or let’s just say, unwise) use of words.

I don’t remember John Kerry (or John Edwards) criticizing President Bush on his umpteen cases of Bushisms. We understand his limitations and respect him for who he is and not for what he misspeaks.

Oh, and in case you were wondering, your partner in crime apparently likes to use the sensitive word in a somewhat similar context as well. Not that I really care. To me these are but mindless asinine attempts to take focus away from things which are far more important.

As a true gentleman that I know you would like Americans to think you are, let us try to steer clear of this mindless (and stupid, I might hastily add) mudslinging, and talk more about what goodies does Bush-Cheney ticket have in store for average Americans in their second term in White House.

That is, if you get one

- Anand
