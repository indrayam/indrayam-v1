To the self-proclaimed political pundits on television/cable: If you think Senator Kerry does not have a clear message..

Posted : September 23, 2004 at 12:55 am [America/Los_Angeles]

..I have a simple solution for you:

“You’ll know once you stop yakking around as a political pundit and care to listen.”

Anyone reading my blog knows that I too had my fair share of concerns around Senator Kerry’s Iraq War and War on Terror position. Not any more though. This week he laid out a very comprehensive plan for Iraq as well as War on Terror. He has been talking about his Economic and Health Care plans during the stump speeches for some time now.

So, the question really is, “Are you paying attention?”

- Anand
