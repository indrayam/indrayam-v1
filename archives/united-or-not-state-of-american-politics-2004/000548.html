Spinsanity galore on Kerry’s speech..:-(

Posted : September 20, 2004 at 7:38 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

No surprise here. Examples:

  • CNN.com does not have the news on it’s main web site (at 19:19 hrs PDT). They had a one-liner in the morning.
  • MSNBC.com had it as it’s main news for most of the day. Titled: Kerry rips Bush over Iraq
  • AP: Kerry Accuses Bush of Incompetence on Iraq. However, the article starts off like this:

    By RON FOURNIER, AP Political Writer

    NEW YORK -
    Staking out new ground on Iraq (news - web sites), Sen. John Kerry (news - web sites) said Monday he would not have overthrown Saddam Hussein (news - web sites) had he been in the White House, and he accused President Bush (news - web sites) of “stubborn incompetence,” dishonesty and colossal failures of judgment. Bush said Kerry was flip-flopping.

    I don’t think “…he (Senator Kerry) would not have overthrown Saddam..” is a fair characterization. His exact words were:

    Saddam Hussein was a brutal dictator who deserves his own special place in hell. But that was not, in itself, a reason to go to war. The satisfaction we take in his downfall does not hide this fact: we have traded a dictator for a chaos that has left America less secure.

    Now that sounds better and correct too. Moreover, if Mr. Ron Fourner can quote President Bush, I think he should have quoted Senator Kerry too.

  • ABCNews.com: Pulling No Punches Kerry Promises to ‘Take the Gloves Off’ — but How Many Does He Have Left?
  • CBSNews.com: Kerry lambasts Bush on War
  • Foxnews.com: Main headlines include ‘Rather, I am sorry’ and ‘Bush: Kerry’s Waffling’. A side-note: Kerry says Bush actions in Iraq have divided U.S. friends, united enemies
  • WashingtonPost.com: Kerry Sharpens Attack On Bush and Iraq War
  • NYTimes.com: In Harshest Critique Yet, Kerry Attacks Bush Over War in Iraq

I have no doubt that the Evening news, the Cable channel shows and our good friends on most of the conservative Radio airwaves would have done the same - ignore it completely or slam it as yet another incident of waffling.

Could’ve been better. Oh well..

Update:
Heard that ABC Evening News (by Peter Jennings) led the news with Kerry’s speech. Nice!
