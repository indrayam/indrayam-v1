First on the ribbon/medal thing. I’m not 100% sure, but I believe that every medal comes with a ribbon. When they wear their “formal uniforms” (think the real “dressy” Marine uniforms) they wear medals. When they are in “non-formal” (think khaki uniforms) they wear ribbons.

On to Kerry’s “medal tossing”. Yes, he fought in the war. Yes, the government awarded him medals for what he did. Yes, he decided that in protest to the war he would “toss them”.

I feel he is now making a mockery out of himself by now acting very proud of himself and his service in Vietnam and trying to use that to get him into the White House. Now, after disrespecting all the soldiers that fought in the war by protesting it, he’s also disrespecting everyone that protested the war by using his service time as a postive.
