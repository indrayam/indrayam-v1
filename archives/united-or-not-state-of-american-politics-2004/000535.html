My Sunday Prayer

Posted : September 19, 2004 at 10:01 am [America/Los_Angeles]

It happened in 2002. The media mavericks, the radio talkshow rabble-rousers and the “we-are-right-cause-we-say-so” hawks led the majority of this country into believing that Iraq is an imminent threat and is part of the War on Terror. They succeeded in pursuading majority of this country into believing thier story. However, they were WRONG.

It’s happening again. This time they’re trying to sell to the American population that “they-have-the-right-answers” and their challenger is “liar, flip-flopper, weak, and plans on prohibiting the Bible if he wins”. And once again, they seem to be winning. However, they are WRONG again!

So, this Sunday morning, I pray that the good decent Americans living across this country have the wisdom and the strength to see through the political chicanery that is being unleashed upon their minds and souls every day. That they take the time to study the issues of this Presidential Elections and then give it their best shot come Nov. 2nd, 2004. If not for themselves and their families, for the many who’ve paid the ultimate price (and continue to do so as I write this) in these past four years.

May God really bless the United States of America.

- Anand
