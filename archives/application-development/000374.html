[ANN]: W.bloggar 4, Spring Live & Equinox. Sweet!

Posted : July 10, 2004 at 9:45 am [America/Los_Angeles]


Here are the links, peppered with my comments:

  • w.bloggar 4: Ofcourse, right now it’s a beta release only for donors and CMS developers. Until last night, I was neither. Today I am a donor Thanks Marcelo. You rock!
  • This post is being authored using w.bloggar 4. I plan on testing this with Movable Type and WordPress in the days and weeks to come. If the tool works with WP, my move from MT 2 WP will officially begin
  • Matt The-Spring-King Raible announced late last evening that he was launching AppFuse Light 1.0 a.k.a Equinox. I can’t wait to take it for a spin, but if it’s even close to what I think it is, Matt has a winner on his hand. Period. Although it is Struts, Spring and Hibernate based, I would like to see if I can come up with a WW2, Spring and iBatis/Hibernate version. Needless to say, great stuff Matt!
  • Matt also announced the launch of the ERP chapters of Spring Live. Coupled with Rod’s new book Expert One-on-One J2EE Development without EJB that arrived in my mail yesterday, I think I have everything that I need (for now) to get rolling with Spring.

A perfect morning in Santa Clara, CA, just got even more beautiful!

- Anand
