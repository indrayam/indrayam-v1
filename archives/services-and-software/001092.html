First Google Desktop 2, now Google Talk..

Posted : August 23, 2005 at 6:27 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


Google Desktop 2 (w Sidebar)



Connecting to Google using any Jabber client

Google’s voice-enabled IM client is coming. As early as tomorrow! Let the fireworks begin. Rumor is that all you will need to connect to Google’s IM service is any Jabber-ready client and your GMail address. Also, you may want to check out Google’s lastest Desktop Search tool, especially if you don’t have Konfabulator running on your desktop.
