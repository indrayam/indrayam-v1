Measuring traffic to your blog WITHOUT having access to your web server logs

Posted : July 13, 2004 at 10:54 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Measuring traffic to your web site/blog using softwares like AWStats is not that difficult.

However, if you don’t consider yourself geeky enough to go through all that trouble or if you just don’t have access to your web server logs, this free service might be just what you have been looking for. All it really involves is adding a one liner Javascript
to the pages you want to capture metrics on. Of course, if you use a common header/footer for your entire site, enabling this service to measure traffic to your entire site is trivial.

I’ve been using this service (just for kicks) for a few days now and it has worked pretty well. It’s not as comprehensive as AWStats, but it sure does get the job done and also has one metric which I don’t think AWStats has - Resolutions. Basically, it displays information regarding the resolution settings of your visitors monitor.

Note:

A few drawbacks of this approach:

  • This service will not capture hits to your XML feeds. As most bloggers know, that’s a very big chunk of your traffic
  • There is a performance hit every time someone visits your site.
