..set one up yourself

Got an email from a colleague this morning:

Team,

I have a couple of invites for the beta Gmail account. If you are interested
let me know and I"ll forward you the invite  .

The funny thing is, thanks to my little Postfix project, I had just finished setting up and customizing SquirrelMail last night. Runs only over HTTPS, backended by my very own email platform, no worries of text or no-text Ads and I get to have way, way more than 1 GB space, thanks to ServerMatrix

So, to Gmail or not to Gmail: that’s my conundrum.

I think I will pass
