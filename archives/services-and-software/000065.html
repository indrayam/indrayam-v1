TOAD for MySQL is indeed getting better

Posted : May 10, 2004 at 12:07 am [America/Los_Angeles]

I had downloaded this one a few weeks back and the version appeared somewhat flaky to me. However, this morning I decided to download it once again (this time for my home computer) and I must say that I have been impressed so far. This software is really coming along very well. I sure hope the guys at Quest at least keep a somewhat pruned down version available for free (as they’ve in the TOAD for Oracle space).

If you work with MySQL, take this one for a test-drive. You will like it.

Note: I have tried it with MySQL 4.0.18. Will be nice to see if it works with 4.1 or 5.0 alpha. I know the previous version did not.
