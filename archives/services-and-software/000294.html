Two things I wish my alma mater had NOT taught me

Posted : June 18, 2004 at 12:15 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Pine and Pico!

If you’ve ever done schooling in States, chances are you know what Pine and Pico is. I have nothing against these softwares. In fact, I think they’re both extremely well written softwares. The problem is, they’re are really meant for students or folks who really don’t want to be on a Unix system, but have to for one reason or another. In other words, they’re truly meant for rookies.

I went to ASU to come out as an IT professional, which I did, thank you very much. However, there are times when I fervently wish that at the time I had started school, someone would have pulled me aside and said - “Son, you need to stay away from Pico and Pine. They are for students majoring in Arts or Communications, not would-be IT professionals. Try Vim as your text editor and Mutt as your email client. You will be glad you did.”

Since I had no such angel whispering in my ears, I am starting the switch now. Pine to Mutt has been a breeze. Vim, well, it might be a while before I can say I have nailed it.

Here’s the moral of the story:



Be wise about what you decide to learn. Take a moment to make sure that it will all be worth your while.

Note:

Didn’t mean to pick on Arts or Communications majors. Could not think of any other group that would be a better fit in making my point

- Anand
