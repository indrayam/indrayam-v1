Konfabulator! Enough said..:-)

Posted : August 17, 2005 at 6:27 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Konfabulator rocks!! Not sure why I had not tried it sooner. What you see above is the right-hand side of my desktop. What are these you ask? Widgets. Konfabulator Widgets to be precise. Really slick widgets which apparently are fairly simple to build. I hear you can build them using Javascript. Definitely need to look into it. Also, in case you’re wondering, that little picture frame that you see in the middle, that’s a “dynamic” picture frame that pulls an image every minute (configurable ofcourse) from my Flickr library. So, do you know any widget that you’d like to recommend.

Btw, both Konfabulator and Flickr are part of Yahoo. Nice steal fellas.
