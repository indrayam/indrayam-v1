YouSendIt: Interesting little service that I came across

Posted : May 13, 2004 at 5:15 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

The idea is simple:

You want to send a large file (upto 1 GB in size) to someone and you cannot as there are size limitations with most public (and even corporate or private) Inboxes. So, what do you do? Folks at YouSendIt.com think you should use their service.

Sounds interesting. However, I still think downloading a file with a size of around 1 GB over the last mile (which is generally dial-up, although DSL & Cable are fast catching up) is impractical. Unless you live in places like South Korea where the download speeds are pretty amazing I have heard. Anyway, thought you might find this interesting.
