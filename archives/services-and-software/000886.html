Cleaning up my Systray using Trillian Pro!

Posted : January 15, 2005 at 2:16 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

I have been resisting installing Trillian as the client for all my instant messaging needs for a long time. Don’t ask me why. It probably had more to do with the fact that I trusted the “source” software providers more than anything else.

However, with the number of IM clients ballooning in my Systray (Yahoo, MSN and Sametime) and hearing good things about the latest Trillian Pro 3 from my colleagues and even my brother, I decided it was time to take the plunge.

Here’s what I’ve installed so far:

  • Trillian Pro 3
  • World Clock plugin
  • Sametime plugin for Trillian

I tried the GMail notifier plugin, but had to dump it in favor of Google’s GMail notifier as it did not really work. Frankly, I would like to get Trillian’s GMail’s notifier plugin working as it would mean one less Systray crap to deal with.

Here are my favorite features of Trillian:

  • Single IM Client. Period!
  • Activity History across all IMs, even if the IM provider’s software natively does not support Activity History. You can, it seems, also archive Audio and Video chats!
  • Configure Trillian to show a systray pop-up when your buddy’s online status changes.
  • Plugin friendly Architecture

- Anand
