Movable Type 2.6x to Word Press 2.1

Posted : January 25, 2007 at 10:15 pm [America/Los_Angeles]


 

I’ve been rather quiet for the past few days. Not a big surprise. It’s not that I have been blogging every day for quite some time now. However, believe it or not, I actually have been working on the blog. So, what was I doing? Well, something that I first talked about in July of 2004. And I talked about again and again and again, but I just could not get myself around to doing anything about it. Why am I talking about it today? Well, let’s just say I am not trying to make any excuses for my rather long procrastination. However, I think the wait is finally over..almost.

Thanks to some much needed push from within (and a deal with my wife), I found myself pouring over Wordpress. Result: I will be launching this blog running under WordPress in a few days. I already have it up and running. The reason I can’t go live on it yet is because I have other blogs on the same LAMP instance that I need to take care of.

So what will be in it for you? Well, if I have done this right, you will not notice a thing. Well, almost. You will definitely see a few changes, some new features, but overall, it would look and feel the same with all my previous links working flawlessly. And if I am really in the mood, I might even try to document (or give detailed pointers) on how I pulled it off.

- Anand
