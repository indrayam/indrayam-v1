Command Prompt Explorer Bar: Nice tool indeed

Posted : May 24, 2004 at 11:10 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

Came across this nice little tool. Basically, think of it as an ‘embedded DOS-prompt’ available from within the Explorer view. Just hit Ctrl+M while you’re in a folder in the Explorer view, and you’ve an embedded DOS prompt at your service. Once you’re done with your command-line work, hit Esc followed by Ctrl+M, and you’re back to your regular Explorer view.
