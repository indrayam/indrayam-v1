del.icio.us gets AJAXed!

Posted : June 27, 2005 at 6:10 pm [America/Los_Angeles]

[via gigaom: del.icio.us direc.tor]

Well, sorta. If you’ve not heard about it, and use del.icio.us service a lot, there’s a new bookmarklet in town. Called del.icio.us director, it’s a nifty client-side web service broker built as a Javascript bookmarklet leveraging XML & XSL capabilities of modern browsers (IE & Firefox), del.icio.us REST-based APIs and AJAX! Take it for a spin. You’ll luv it. I promise.
