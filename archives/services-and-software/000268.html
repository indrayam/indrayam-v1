FishEye: See your code in a whole new light

Posted : June 11, 2004 at 9:01 am [America/Los_Angeles]

Your source code repository contains an abundance of valuable information. This is not always easy to extract, comprehend and keep up to date. FishEye painlessly opens your repository and helps you get the perspective you need to better understand your changing source.

I am not sure how long ago it was, but I distinctly remember my first reaction : Wow! If you’ve ever dabbled with CVS, either hosting code repositories for others or using one yourself, you cannot not be impressed by what FishEye cooks up. Check out their demo instances, showing Apache Ant’s and Jakarta Cactus’s repositories.

So, why am i blogging about it. Did I get a chance to actually run it against my own CVS repository. Well, not really. But I soon will. Thanks to this email that I received last night

Hi,

Some time ago you requested to be part of the FishEye beta-program. We
are now writing to invite you to join the next round of beta testing,
if you are still interested.

You will soon receive another email with details on how to download
the beta. (If you do not get this email, please let us know, as some
people report that such emails are discarded as spam.)

Thanks for you interest in FishEye, we look forward to any feedback
you may have.

Regards,
The FishEye Team
http://www.thecortex.net/fisheye

Nice

- Anand
